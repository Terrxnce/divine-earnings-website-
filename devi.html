What is D.E.V.I?

D.E.V.I identifies institutional market structure — order blocks, breaks of structure, fair value gaps, liquidity sweeps — and only executes when every gate passes. Every decision is deterministic, logged with a coded reason, and fully auditable. Built for prop-firm traders and serious retail traders who want a system they can inspect.

Structure Engine

  • Order blocks, breaks of structure, FVGs, liquidity sweeps
  • M15 entries with H1 trend and H4 context
  • Change of character required on every retest

Safety Gates

  • Hard stop and target inside every order
  • Minimum 1.2:1 reward-to-risk, net of commission
  • 1% risk per trade, daily loss block at −3%, flat at −4.5%

Full Transparency

  • Every evaluation logged with a coded reason
  • Roughly 2,200 setups rejected per day
  • Live dashboard — nothing hidden
