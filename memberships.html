FAQ

What's the difference between Signals and Standard?

With Signals, D.E.V.I sends you its trade setups via Discord and you place the trades yourself. With Standard and Pro, D.E.V.I installs on your machine and places the trades automatically through your MT5 account — no manual input needed.

When will Standard and Pro be available?

D.E.V.I is currently in live testing. Standard and Pro will open to a small beta cohort before full release. Join the waitlist on the D.E.V.I page to be notified.

Does D.E.V.I work with my broker or funded account?

D.E.V.I works with any MT5-compatible account. It is your responsibility to confirm that automated trading is permitted under your account's terms and conditions before using D.E.V.I.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No long-term contracts. Cancel through the client portal at any time with no cancellation fees.

What payment methods do you accept?

All major credit and debit cards through Stripe. Secure checkout, no card details stored on our servers.
